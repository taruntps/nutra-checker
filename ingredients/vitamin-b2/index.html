Frequently asked questions

Is Vitamin B2 permitted in nutraceuticals under FSSAI (India)?

Vitamin B2 is currently permitted for use in health supplements and nutraceuticals under India's FSSAI framework, per FSS (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals…) Regulations 2022. Always verify against the current official regulation for your product and market.

What is the permitted limit of Vitamin B2?

No single numeric limit is listed in the source for Vitamin B2; permitted use is at GMP or as specified. Confirm against the current regulation.

What product category does Vitamin B2 belong to?

Vitamin B2 is classified under: Vitamins.

What is the recommended daily allowance (RDA) of Vitamin B2 in India?

Per ICMR-NIN (2020), the RDA of Vitamin B2 for adult man is 2 mg. RDA is a dietary reference intake and is distinct from the maximum permitted supplement limit.
