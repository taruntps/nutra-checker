Frequently asked questions

Is Chloride permitted in nutraceuticals under FSSAI (India)?

Chloride is currently permitted for use in health supplements and nutraceuticals under India's FSSAI framework, per FSS (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals…) Regulations 2022. Always verify against the current official regulation for your product and market.

What is the permitted dosage of Chloride?

No single numeric limit is listed in the source for Chloride; permitted use is at GMP or as specified. Confirm against the current regulation.

What product category does Chloride belong to?

Chloride is classified under: Minerals.

What is the recommended daily allowance (RDA) of Chloride in India?

Per ICMR-NIN (2020), the RDA of Chloride for adult man is 2300 mg. RDA is a dietary reference intake and is distinct from the maximum permitted supplement limit.
