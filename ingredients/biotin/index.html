Frequently asked questions

Is Biotin permitted in nutraceuticals under FSSAI (India)?

Biotin is currently permitted for use in health supplements and nutraceuticals under India's FSSAI framework, per FSS (Health Supplements, Nutraceuticals…) Regulations 2022. Always verify against the current official regulation for your product and market.

What is the permitted dosage of Biotin?

No single numeric limit is listed in the source for Biotin; permitted use is at GMP or as specified. Confirm against the current regulation.

What product category does Biotin belong to?

Biotin is classified under: Vitamins.

What is the recommended daily allowance (RDA) of Biotin in India?

Per ICMR-NIN (2020), the RDA of Biotin for adult man is 40 mcg. RDA is a dietary reference intake and is distinct from the maximum permitted supplement limit.
